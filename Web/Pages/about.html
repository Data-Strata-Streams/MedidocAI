About MedidocAI

Empowering Pakistan with Medical Intelligence.

Our Mission

MedidocAI was founded with a single goal: to make reliable medical and pharmaceutical information accessible to every citizen of Pakistan. In a country where healthcare information can be complex or hard to reach, we use cutting-edge Artificial Intelligence to provide instant, clear, and accurate medicine data at your fingertips.

Advanced AI

Powered by Gemini 2.5 Flash, our AI understands text, voice, and even prescription images in English and Urdu.

Verified Data

We search a proprietary database of over 20,000+ local brands to ensure you get the most accurate local information.

How It Works

Whether you are a member of the general public looking for affordable generic alternatives, a medical student researching pharmacology, or a healthcare professional needing quick clinical references, MedidocAI adapts to your needs. Our multimodal system allows you to query via text, audio voice notes, or photos of medicine boxes and prescriptions.
